# Fontes das afirmações

As perguntas são sobre histórias, crenças e mitologias; as explicações distinguem tradições históricas de representações ficcionais atuais.

- **Lobisomem:** Daniel Ogden, *The Werewolf in the Ancient World*, Oxford University Press: https://academic.oup.com/book/33458
- **Bruxas:** English Heritage, *A Journey into Witchcraft Beliefs*: https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/learn/histories/journey-into-witchcraft-beliefs/
- **Vampiros:** Smithsonian Magazine, *Meet the Real-Life Vampires of New England and Abroad*: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/meet-the-real-life-vampires-of-new-england-and-abroad-42639093/
- **Fantasma:** Plínio, o Jovem, carta sobre a casa assombrada em Atenas, reproduzida em *Greek and Roman Ghost Stories*: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/17190/17190-h/17190-h.htm
- **Zumbi:** HISTORY, *History of Zombies*: https://www.history.com/articles/history-of-zombies
- **Dragão:** American Museum of Natural History, *Dragon*: https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/ology-cards/277-dragon
